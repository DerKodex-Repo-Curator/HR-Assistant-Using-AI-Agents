COMPANY HR POLICY MANUAL
Version 2.1 — Effective January 2026

====================================
SECTION 2: EMPLOYMENT CONDITIONS
====================================

Section 2.1 — Probation Period
All new employees are subject to a 6-month probation period. During probation, either party may terminate employment with 2 weeks notice. Performance reviews are conducted at 3 months and 6 months. Successful completion of probation is confirmed in writing by the HR department.

Section 2.2 — Working Hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM, Monday to Friday. A 30-minute lunch break is included. Total working hours per week are 40 hours. Flexible start times between 7:30 AM and 10:00 AM may be arranged with manager approval.

Section 2.3 — Dress Code
Business casual attire is expected in the office. Client-facing meetings require business professional attire. Casual Fridays allow jeans and smart casual wear.

====================================
SECTION 3: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS
====================================

Section 3.1 — Pay Schedule
Employees are paid bi-weekly on every other Friday. Direct deposit is mandatory. Pay stubs are available through the employee self-service portal.

Section 3.2 — Health Insurance
Full-time employees are eligible for health insurance from their start date. The company covers 80% of the premium for employee-only coverage and 60% for family coverage. Plans include medical, dental, and vision. Open enrollment occurs annually in November.

Section 3.3 — Retirement Plan
The company offers a 401(k) retirement plan with a 4% employer match. Employees are eligible after 90 days of employment. Vesting is immediate for employee contributions and 3-year graded vesting for employer match.

====================================
SECTION 4: LEAVE POLICIES
====================================

Section 4.1 — Public Holidays
The company observes 10 public holidays per year: New Year's Day, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Columbus Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.

Section 4.2 — Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per year. Leave accrues at 1.67 days per month. Unused leave may be carried over up to a maximum of 5 days into the next calendar year. Carry-over days must be used by March 31. Employees with 5+ years of service receive 25 days. Employees with 10+ years receive 30 days.

Section 4.3 — Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences of 3 or more consecutive days. Unused sick leave does not carry over to the next year. Sick leave may be used for personal illness, medical appointments, or caring for an immediate family member.

Section 4.4 — Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 12 weeks of paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 4 weeks of paid parental leave. Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Additional unpaid leave of up to 4 weeks may be requested.

Section 4.5 — Bereavement Leave
Employees receive 5 days of paid bereavement leave for the death of an immediate family member (spouse, parent, child, sibling). 3 days are provided for extended family members (grandparent, in-law, aunt, uncle).

====================================
SECTION 5: WORKPLACE POLICIES
====================================

Section 5.1 — Work From Home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. A minimum of 3 days per week must be spent in the office. Work from home arrangements must be documented and approved through the HR portal. Employees must maintain a suitable home workspace and reliable internet connection.

Section 5.2 — Overtime
Non-exempt employees are eligible for overtime pay at 1.5 times their regular hourly rate for hours worked beyond 40 per week. All overtime must be pre-approved by the employee's direct manager. Exempt employees are not eligible for overtime pay.

Section 5.3 — Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted annually in December. Mid-year check-ins occur in June. Reviews assess goal achievement, competency development, and alignment with company values. Ratings are used to determine annual salary adjustments and bonus eligibility.

Section 5.4 — Training and Development
The company provides an annual training budget of $2,500 per employee. Employees may use this for courses, conferences, certifications, or books. Requests must be submitted through the learning management system and approved by the manager. Time spent in approved training during working hours is considered work time.
